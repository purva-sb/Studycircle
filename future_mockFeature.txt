PART 1 — RICHER QUESTION METADATA (subject / subtopic / source)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Right now questions have a single `topic` field (free text). To enable real
weakness analysis ("you're 80% in Calculus but 35% in Probability — drill
into Probability → Conditional probability → 20%"), we need a richer tag set.

─── Fields to add to questionsTable ─────────────────────────────────────────
  - subject     (e.g. Mathematics / Physics / Computer Science)
  - subtopic    (e.g. Integration by parts / Friction / Binary trees)
  - source      (e.g. "GATE 2024", "NCERT Ch 5", "PYQ", "Custom")
  - difficulty  (already exists at exam level — could add per-question too)

─── Schema approach ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
RECOMMENDED: Free-text columns with autocomplete from existing values in
the same exam. Don't over-engineer a taxonomy table upfront — wait until
real institutes use it, then normalize.

Pros: ship fast, zero setup friction.
Cons: typos fragment data ("Calculus" vs "calc"). Mitigate with
case-insensitive matching on the analytics side.

─── Manual vs AI tagging ────────────────────────────────────────────────────
BUILD BOTH:
  - Manual: admin fills the 3 fields, or includes them in CSV
  - AI-assisted: "Suggest tags" button in admin form
       → sends question body to Claude Haiku
       → returns {subject, topic, subtopic, difficulty}
       → admin reviews & accepts
       Cost: ~$0.001 per question. Massive time saver for CSV imports
       where teacher left subtopic empty.

─── CSV format extension ────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Add backward-compatible columns:
  kind, body, optA, optB, optC, optD, correct, marks, negMarks,
  section, topic, subject, subtopic, source, explanation

PART 2 — ANALYTICS THAT ATTRACT + HELP STUDENTS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
🪝 ENGAGEMENT — what brings them in & keeps them coming back
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Daily streak
    "🔥 5-day streak — don't break it!" on dashboard.
    Doubles retention in most ed-tech studies. Small effort.

  Shareable WhatsApp result card
    After exam, generate a pretty image with score + rank → wa.me share.
    Viral loop — biggest free acquisition channel for Indian ed-tech.
    Small effort.

  All-India Rank predictor
    "Based on your last 5 mocks, you'd rank #3,450 in actual GATE 2026."
    Single biggest reason students stay. Medium effort.

  Activity heatmap
    GitHub-style "questions solved per day" calendar.
    Small effort.

  Weekly cohort stats
    "1,247 students attempted this exam this week, you beat 73%."
    Small effort.

  Achievement badges
    10 mocks done, 100% in a section, 30-day streak.
    Each unlock = dopamine hit. Medium effort.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
🎯 INSIGHTS — actual learning value
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Subject / Topic / Subtopic strength radar
    Drill-down: Subject → Topic → Subtopic with red/yellow/green bars.
    Needs: subject/subtopic tags (Part 1).

  Time-per-question analysis
    "Avg 3 min on Calculus, but top scorers do it in 1:30."
    Needs: per-question timing (track when student lands on question).

  Mistake patterns (AI classification)
    "70% of your wrongs are silly mistakes (you knew the concept)"
    vs  "you don't understand topic X".
    Needs: subtopic tags + Claude classification, plus 50+ attempts per
           user to calibrate.

  Weak subtopic flag
    List of subtopics with <40% accuracy AND most frequent in PYQ.
    Needs: subtopic tags + PYQ source data.

  Improvement velocity
    "Your score is up 8 marks over the last month."
    Needs: trend analysis (already have score history).

  Concept-gap detector
    "You consistently fail questions on Thermodynamics 2nd law."
    Needs: subtopic tags.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
🔮 PREDICTIVE — gives confidence / drives FOMO
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Cutoff probability
    "78% likely to clear GATE CSE cutoff (Gen)."
    Emotionally massive. Needs real cutoff dataset + historical results.

  Days-to-ready estimate
    "At your current pace, you'll be exam-ready in 42 days."
    Needs: improvement velocity model.

  Best topic to study next
    AI picks based on weakness × frequency-in-past-papers.
    Needs: subtopic tags + PYQ source data + Claude.

  Score trajectory
    "If you keep improving at this rate → 78 marks by exam day."
    Needs: regression line on score trend.

PART 3 — VIDEO / ARTICLE SOLUTIONS PER QUESTION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Each question can have optional solution material:
  • solutionVideoUrl   (https://, YouTube only — youtube.com / youtu.be)
  • solutionArticleUrl (https:// any domain)

YouTube-only for video is a security choice: prevents teachers from
pasting redirect URLs or untrusted hosts. Article URL stays open because
teachers link to their own blogs.

Where they appear:
  • Results page Question Review card (after exam submit) — two buttons:
      📹 Watch Video Solution    📄 Read Article Solution
  • Both open in new tab with rel="noopener noreferrer" (no tab-jacking)
  • Only rendered when URL exists

Admin entry:
  • Two new fields in question form (next to Explanation)
  • Two new CSV columns: solutionVideoUrl, solutionArticleUrl
    (backward-compatible — older CSVs still parse)

PART 4 — COMMUNITY Q&A LINKING (GATEOverflow-style growth loop)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The doubts feature already exists in the codebase (doubtsTable +
DoubtsFeed page). We wire it to mock-test questions so students who get
a question wrong can ask the community, and future students discover the
answer via search.

────────────────────────────────────────
LAYER 1 — Manual linking (~1 hour)
────────────────────────────────────────
  • Add questionId (nullable FK) to doubtsTable
  • Question Review card after exam shows:
      💬 Discuss in Community  — opens doubt-create form with question
                                  body pre-filled
      💬 3 discussions          — if doubts already linked, shows count
  • Doubt detail page shows the linked exam question at top for context

────────────────────────────────────────
LAYER 2 — Auto-recommendations (~2 hours)
────────────────────────────────────────
  Needs subject/subtopic tags (Part 1) to be useful.

  When viewing a question's solution, query doubts where:
    - questionId matches (direct link), OR
    - topic / subtopic / subject match
  Rank by upvotes + recency. Show top 3 as "Related Discussions".

  Same logic on the doubt detail page itself: show 3 other related doubts.

────────────────────────────────────────
LAYER 3 — SEO play (~1.5 hours, only after ~100 doubts exist)
────────────────────────────────────────
  • Make doubt URLs public + indexable: /doubts/:id
  • OpenGraph + Twitter card meta tags (title + first 200 chars of body)
  • sitemap.xml listing all public doubts
  • Result: Google indexes thousands of discussed GATE questions →
    organic discovery → free traffic

The compounding effect:
    Student takes mock → gets Q wrong → reads explanation
      → still confused → clicks "Discuss"
      → community answers → answer helps next 100 students
      → Google indexes the thread → 1000 more students discover the site
      → some sign up → take mocks → cycle repeats

This is Stack Overflow's growth loop applied to exam prep.

PART 4.5 — AI FEATURES (mock-test scope only)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

────────────────────────────────────────
STUDENT-SIDE (during / after mock test)
────────────────────────────────────────

  "Explain this question"           Haiku    🔥 huge
    After exam, on any wrong question. One button → Claude generates
    step-by-step solution. Used when admin didn't write an explanation,
    or as supplement when they did.

  AI Doubt Solver                   Sonnet   🔥 huge
    After exam, on any question card. "I still don't get it" opens a
    chat thread with Claude about that specific question. Conversation
    scoped to the question + correct answer + student's wrong answer.

  AI weakness narrative             Sonnet   high
    On results page. Plain-English paragraph:
      "You're strong in Calculus (85%) but Probability is dragging you
       down (35%). Focus next 2 weeks on conditional probability."
    Generated from result.topicBreakdown + score data.

  "Practice similar"                Haiku    medium
    On a wrong-question card. Generates 5 similar questions on the
    same subtopic for a mini-mock. Auto-creates a new attempt.

  Next mock recommendation          Haiku    medium
    After exam ends. "Based on this result, take Mock #4 next — it
    focuses on your weak topics." Ranks existing mocks by overlap
    with weakness topics.

────────────────────────────────────────
ADMIN-SIDE (creating mock tests)
────────────────────────────────────────

  Auto-tag                          Haiku    🔥 huge
    Question form. "Suggest tags" button fills subject/topic/subtopic/
    difficulty from the question body. Single biggest admin time-saver.
    Unlocks the entire analytics layer.

  Generate explanation              Haiku    🔥 huge
    Question form. Admin enters Q + correct answer → AI drafts the
    explanation. Admin reviews and edits before save.

  Generate distractors              Haiku    high
    Question form (MCQ/MSQ only). Admin enters Q + correct option →
    AI generates 3 wrong-but-plausible options.

  Generate questions                Sonnet   high
    New "Generate Questions" button per exam. Admin types prompt:
      "10 hard NAT questions on Probability, GATE CSE syllabus"
    → 10 draft questions added to the exam for review.

  Difficulty estimator              Haiku    medium
    Question form. Predicts easy / medium / hard from the body alone.

  CSV auto-fill                     Haiku    huge
    CSV import preview. Detects rows with missing tags or explanations,
    fills them before insert. Massive for institutes uploading old
    paper banks where metadata is sparse.

  OCR past papers                   Vision   huge for B2B
    New upload flow. Scan printed GATE/JEE PDF or image → extract
    questions to a draft exam. Differentiator vs WhatsApp + paper.

  Duplicate detector                Haiku    medium
    On question add. Flags if a near-duplicate already exists in this
    exam (or another exam by the same teacher).

────────────────────────────────────────
BUSINESS / COST MODEL
────────────────────────────────────────

  Pricing approach:
    • Free tier: 10 AI-doubt-solver calls per day, basic auto-tagging
    • Student Pro (~₹99/mo): unlimited AI, chat tutor, study plans
    • Institute tier: AI question gen + OCR + bulk auto-tagging
      (this is what makes institutes pay vs WhatsApp + paper)

  Anthropic API cost estimate at ~10K students:
    • Heavy AI users: ~₹15/month each
    • Pro tier ₹99/mo → ~80% margin

  Recommended FIRST 3 AI features to ship:
    1. Auto-tag (admin)         — saves hours, unlocks analytics
    2. Generate explanation     — solves "no explanation" problem
    3. AI weakness narrative    — post-exam "why this matters"

  Together = product feels like an intelligent tutor.

PART 5 — OPEN SECURITY ITEMS (deferred from earlier audit)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

These are known limitations from the security audit. Server-side locking
of sectional progress is the biggest one — sectional timer is currently
client-side only, a determined student with DevTools can answer any
question in any section within the overall time window.

  • Server-side sectional locking
      Backend needs to track currentSectionIdx per attempt and reject
      answers to questions outside the unlocked section. Requires:
      - DB column on examAttemptsTable: currentSectionIdx
      - Endpoint: POST /attempts/:id/advance-section
      - Reject /answer if question.sectionId is in locked sections

  • Race condition on attemptCount increment
      `attemptCount: e.attemptCount + 1` is read-then-write.
      Fix: use sql`${examsTable.attemptCount} + 1` for atomic increment.

  • Section delete during active attempts
      Block section deletion if any in-progress attempt exists.

  • Leaderboard opt-out
      Currently always shows displayName + avatar of all participants.
      Add a per-user privacy setting if needed later.

PART 6 — NORTH STAR (why this matters)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

CircleTest is B2B + B2C: institutes pay for the authoring/distribution
pipeline, students use it free and drive viral/gamified growth.

The analytics features above are what separate CircleTest from being
"just another exam platform" — they are the reason a student tells their
friend "use CircleTest" instead of "use Testbook".

The order matters:
  1. Tags first → makes everything else possible
  2. Insights second → makes the tool feel intelligent
  3. Predictions third → makes the tool feel essential

Each phase compounds on the previous. Don't skip Phase 1 to chase Phase 3.
